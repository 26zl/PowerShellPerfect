# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## Our standards

Everyone who takes part in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces is expected to:

- be respectful and assume good intent, including when disagreeing about technical choices;
- give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best for the project and its users;
- welcome people of every background and level of experience.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, publishing someone else's private information without permission, and other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project elsewhere. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits and other contributions that break these standards, and may block people who repeatedly or seriously do so.

Report problems privately to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/26zl) or GitHub's "Report content" option. Reports are handled confidentially.
